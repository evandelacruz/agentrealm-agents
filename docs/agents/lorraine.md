# Ladder Lorraine

**Short name:** `lorraine`. **Package:** `python/ladder/` (M14). Run from `python/` with `python3 -m ladder create|run|status <profile>`, the same commands and profile files as Gary. Her command-line parser and `status` self line are the ADK's (`agentrealm_adk.cli`, A149), as Gary's are. `run` plays on her own loop over the ADK (`ladder/play.py`, A141), with `--no-planner` for the test mode: each round trip her reflexes first, then the walk under way, then the op for the tree's goal, carried out by `ladder/act.py` on the ADK's executor and pathing.

**Plays to:** levels cleared per hour. Everything else is a means to that.

**How it thinks.** One goal tree, held as data (`ladder/goals.py`, A139), walked from the top every turn: beat the world; beat the next level; under it, hold what it requires (the entrance's need: a key, sandals, a bomb) and ready for its risk (heals, weapon, armor against the level's danger); under both, hold the gems for what's missing; under that, gather; under that, stay alive. Each node has a readiness predicate computed from state and the op that works it. The walk goes into the first child that is not ready; a node whose children are all ready is the turn's goal. Risk is one function (`ladder/readiness.py` `risk_budget`): health plus the heals carried against the expected cost of the goal, Gary's win estimate reused, with a valuable goal (a level and its boss, a gem cache) allowed to cost three quarters and an ordinary one half. "Ready for its risk" is that function on the level, "stay alive" on the goal being worked (counting only the heals she can use now), and her reflexes and walks read it too. At the turn's goal the planner is asked one question (`ladder/ask.py`, A140): given that node, the state its predicate reads, the ops that can work it and the game reference, the op to run now, or `cannot` with why and the state line it waits on. No stack and no stage prose. A `cannot` blocks the node with its reason and the walk descends past it. It may wait only on a line that changes when the situation does: `held`, `levels_cleared`, `sellers`, `entrance_needs`, `gems N` (N gems held) or `ticks N` (a shorter expiry); never where she stands, her health or the readiness text, which change every step or hit. The block lifts when what it waits on comes, when its blocker can have changed (A169, below), or lapses after `BLOCK_TICKS`, and the next walk asks afresh. A `gems N` already held when the `cannot` comes is no wait: the node is blocked to its expiry and she asks once more, saying the condition is already met. She asks only when the goal or that watched state changes, or after an ask with no answer once the backoff allows, never every tick; with no answer (the budget spent, backing off, a failed call) the tree's own op runs. The call itself is the ADK's (`agentrealm_adk.llm`): the providers, call budget, backoff and reply parsing Gary's planner uses, never his prompt. The call runs off the tick thread, so a slow or hung one never holds up a reflex: until its reply is back the tree's own op runs. The op is then carried out (`ladder/act.py`, A141): one table maps each op to its walk and intents; every walk is the ADK's guided walk (its stuck ladder and give-ups) sent as a paced `Step` queue, keeping off the ground of every known hostile but the ones a fight with which fits her margin now; an op over (carried out, or nothing left it can do) is not run again for that goal. She never holds in silence (A168): a walk with no step gives its target up for the ADK's backoff and is over; an op with many targets (gather, explore) takes the next-best one that has a step, and gather ranks a target beside known hostiles last, taking it only when her risk budget covers them all; an op that sends nothing the same way twice is over (a round with no target that has a step), but a wait the clock clears (a weapon cooldown, her place not read yet, occupants on her way) never ends it and is no stall; and a stall guard counts the rounds the op sends nothing with no reflex holding, blocking the goal after `STALL_ROUNDS` of them (a round waiting on the planner's answer about the goal is no stall), after which the planner is asked once with why (about the blocked node itself when every way down is blocked by her own blocks). A blocked goal never idles her (A169): a blocked node's siblings come first in tree order, and only then, below the block, the first node not ready anywhere under it, under a ready node too; and "gather" always has an op (up to what is missing, else a few gems past what she holds), so the tree keeps working its other nodes in order; every block, a `cannot` or her own, is retried once its blocker can have changed (the region she stands in, what she carries, or whether her health covers what is ahead on her risk function, is not what the planner saw, a walk given up for the goal may be walked again, or it lapses), and the next walk asks again. A walk given up is walked again only once its backoff has ended and the ground round it has changed (A171: cells seen there, a block opened, an occupant gone, a capability gained; never time alone, and the rule is the ADK's, Gary's too); while it holds, the goal stays blocked, her region alone changing nothing, and the planner is asked once for another way. With no goal at all while a block stands she always asks the planner about the blocked node, with why; exploring only fills the wait for the answer. A target her walk gave up is not chosen again while its give-up holds: her buy skips a seller given up, the planner is shown each given-up target with why and what lifts it, and an answer that walks back to one is not run. Gather weighs every target by its net, its value less its walk's gems (A170, A172): grass at the gems a cut drops there, and a region's gem-cache oddity a held capability may break, worth its expected cache (what it has dropped, the Manual's 5, 7 and 10 at first), once its regrow timer has run and no cache lies on it; one she cannot break is never walked to, and the planner is shown what it needs. Occupants are no obstacle: the ADK's walk goes round them or waits for them, a wait that never fails its stuck window, so only terrain gives a target up; her walk waits on them as a wait the clock clears, up to the ADK's cap for every walk (`OCCUPANT_WAIT_TICKS`), then is over without giving the target up; a person or shelf is reached from the free cell within reach nearest her, never by standing on their cell. Every target has one value in gems, the ADK's (`agentrealm_adk/value.py`, A170): a gem its count, food the health it would give back at the cheapest potion's gems per health (nothing at full health), a bush its berry times the drop chance she learns from her cuts, a life a fixed weight, a chest what it holds that fits her pack (her own death chests too, remembered across runs; one never opened at what opened chests held on average). A step on clear ground costs 0.1 gem; hostile ground, hazards and obstacles add the cost grid's surcharges at that rate. Gather works the target with the highest net (value less its walk); every walk, whatever op sent it, goes by a find whose value beats its added cost, and an attack or a threat her margin cannot pay for declines it; and "stay alive" heals from the cheapest source with its walk counted, food near before a potion. Each tick record names the goal node, the goals blocked and why, the op, the act's result and the reflex that held the round. Above the op run her reflexes (`ladder/reflexes.py`): sync, downed, flee, retreat, heal and fight back, each on one number, her margin now (`readiness.margin_now`: the hostiles that can reach her, valued as the goal she works), never a threshold of its own. Every send goes through the same water gate as Gary's (`fording.ford_gate`). Only sync and downed hold the round with nothing to send; any other reflex with nothing to send yields to the next, then to the op. Flee and retreat head for one refuge, the nearest safe block she gets to before any hostile, each priced as a chaser. A reflex never sets a goal: it holds the round, and the next walk of the tree decides. `ladder status` prints the tree's readiness from the self and world reads; it never makes a round trip, but do not run it beside a live run on the same character, whose call budget its reads spend.

**Temperament.** Fixed by the tree, not by knobs: she climbs as soon as she is ready and descends only as far as she must. The few knobs she has name a readiness bar (health fraction, gems in hand, weapon class) and nothing else.

**Will not.** Add a rule for a situation (A87, at zero from day one). Import anything of Gary's (`agentrealm_agent`), his planner's prompt included. Gather, shop or explore for its own sake: she gathers past what is missing only while what is above is blocked, for the buys to come, and explores with no goal only while a block stands.

**Lineage.** Built on the agent ADK, `python/agentrealm_adk/` (client, world model, pathing, executor, supplies, the planner call plumbing in `llm`), the plumbing grown in Gary and moved out of him (A149), to test one claim: a goal tree with the situational calls delegated to the planner beats accreted rules. Becomes the reference agent if the metrics say so (A143).
