# Ladder Lorraine

**Short name:** `lorraine`. **Package:** `python/ladder/` (M14). Run from `python/` with `python3 -m ladder create|run|status <profile>`, the same commands and profile files as Gary. Her command-line parser and `status` self line are the ADK's (`agentrealm_adk.cli`, A149), as Gary's are. `run` plays her goal tree (`ladder/play.py`, A140), with `--no-planner` for the test mode. How it carries out an op is minimal until A141: the sync, wake and flee are Stan's (`starter_agent`), an op whose x, y is the cell she goes to (`travel`, `fetch_item`, `enter_level`) is walked to over known ground on this map and is done once she stands there, any other op explores as Stan does, and the planner's question is asked on the tick thread, so play waits while a call is out. A call that hangs stalls her, flee included, up to the provider client's timeout: 600 s in the `anthropic` SDK. A141 takes the call off the tick thread. That tie is temporary and is her one import of Stan.

**Plays to:** levels cleared per hour. Everything else is a means to that.

**How it thinks.** One goal tree, held as data (`ladder/goals.py`, A139), walked from the top every turn: beat the world; beat the next level; under it, hold what it requires (the entrance's need: a key, sandals, a bomb) and ready for its risk (heals, weapon, armor against the level's danger); under both, hold the gems for what's missing; under that, gather; under that, stay alive. Each node has a readiness predicate computed from state and the op that works it. The walk goes into the first child that is not ready; a node whose children are all ready is the turn's goal. Risk is one function (`ladder/readiness.py` `risk_budget`): health plus the heals carried against the expected cost of the goal, Gary's win estimate reused, with a valuable goal (a level and its boss, a gem cache) allowed to cost three quarters and an ordinary one half. "Ready for its risk" is that function on the level, "stay alive" on the goal being worked (counting only the heals she can use now), and her reflexes will read it too. At the turn's goal the planner is asked one question (`ladder/ask.py`, A140): given that node, the state its predicate reads, the ops that can work it and the game reference, the op to run now, or `cannot` with why and the state line it waits on. No stack and no stage prose. A `cannot` blocks the node with its reason and the walk descends past it. It may wait only on a line that changes when the situation does: `held`, `levels_cleared`, `sellers`, `entrance_needs`, `gems N` (N gems held) or `ticks N` (a shorter expiry); never where she stands, her health or the readiness text, which change every step or hit. The block lifts when what it waits on comes, or lapses after `BLOCK_TICKS`, and the next walk asks afresh. A `gems N` already held when the `cannot` comes is no wait: the node is blocked to its expiry and she asks once more, saying the condition is already met. She asks only when the goal or that watched state changes, or after an ask with no answer once the backoff allows, never every tick; with no answer (the budget spent, backing off, a failed call) the tree's own op runs. The call itself is the ADK's (`agentrealm_adk.llm`): the providers, call budget, backoff and reply parsing Gary's planner uses, never his prompt. The executor and reflexes then carry out the op (A141). `ladder status` prints the tree's readiness from the self and world reads; it never makes a round trip, but do not run it beside a live run on the same character, whose call budget its reads spend.

**Temperament.** Fixed by the tree, not by knobs: she climbs as soon as she is ready and descends only as far as she must. The few knobs she has name a readiness bar (health fraction, gems in hand, weapon class) and nothing else.

**Will not.** Add a rule for a situation (A87, at zero from day one). Import anything of Gary's (`agentrealm_agent`), his planner's prompt included. Gather, shop or explore for its own sake.

**Lineage.** Built on the agent ADK, `python/agentrealm_adk/` (client, world model, pathing, executor, supplies, the planner call plumbing in `llm`), the plumbing grown in Gary and moved out of him (A149), to test one claim: a goal tree with the situational calls delegated to the planner beats accreted rules. Becomes the reference agent if the metrics say so (A143).
