# A152: the agent against the published manual

On 2026-10-12 the Manual (https://agentrealm.gg/docs/manual) gained the core play rules: where a supply works (armed, worn, carried), what waits on a cooldown, food and potion heals, no natural regeneration, the chest on death, levels and boss fights, hostiles, safe zones, regrowth and oddities, and shops. [`GAME_NOTES.md`](../GAME_NOTES.md) now cites them. This file lists every place the agent's code contradicts the Manual, or still infers or works around something it now states.

Line numbers are on this PR's head. "M §11 X" is Manual §11, sub-heading X; "SR" is the Supplies reference. **Action** is one of: *fixed here* (a constant, docstring or contract line), *follow-up A1nn* (a behaviour change, filed in PLAN.md and listed below, not done here), *policy* (the agent's own choice, kept), or *agrees* (checked, nothing to do).

## Proposed follow-up items

Filed in PLAN.md as A153–A164 (F1–F12 in order); the code comments cite those IDs.

Each is rule-shaped (A87): one rule in game terms, applied wherever it reaches, with no branch for a situation.

- **A153 (F1). Only a weapon swing waits on the attack cooldown.** A `Use` is paced by the armed weapon's cooldown only when it is a weapon's swing at a character or NPC, or a weapon's break of a block (`executor.pacing`); a consumable's, tool's or trap's `Use`, and the `Arm` before it, go out on the next tick. `last_use_tick` moves only on an applied weapon `Use`. A refused drink retries on its own constant; a tool's break costs no cooldown in `break_memory`.
- **A154 (F2). Each supply is in one place.** Drop the A76 checks for the armed supply in `held` (`healing.potion_count`, `supply_refusals.in_hand`, `break_memory.held_supplies`, which today hides the armed supply when a held one shares its code); `droppable_supplies` offers armed and worn supplies too, and `fording._loadout_after` models a `Drop` of a worn one.
- **A155 (F3). Food is eaten on pickup, never carried.** Heal's carried-food branch, `held_heal`'s food and the A24 heal learning go; heals come from the Supplies reference. Food takes no pack room (`pack.make_room`), a `fetch_item` of food is done when taken, and Pickup and the contract leave food lying until the character is hurt by at least its heal. A potion is never sent at full health.
- **A156 (F4). Hearts come from the Supplies reference.** A supply of class `life` is a life (`heart`); `learn_life_code` and `life_on_pickup` retire. Detour and Pickup then go for hearts on sight; their scores need a check.
- **A157 (F5). A death resets the chest.** The applied chest is cleared on `Died` and, at load, when the character died since it was saved; a chest `Use` that is `applied` sets it and one that is `applied_no_effect` does not.
- **A158 (F6). A dropped priced supply costs its price again, anywhere.** The pack rule prices a drop of a priced subtype as forfeiting its price, on any cell; `drop_makes_stock` and the step off a shop cell go; a priced supply the character dropped is not recorded as a shop.
- **A159 (F7). Shop stock returns.** A shop cell seen without the item is sold out for two minutes (`RESTOCK_SECONDS`), not forgotten; the sale lapses only on a longer absence.
- **A160 (F8). An odd block may need any of the five.** Break refuses a `break_block`'s capability on a known type only when the block is not one State `oddities` lists; `may_break_with` on an oddity is every capability held.
- **A161 (F9). Gather fells trees.** With a chop tool armed or held, a wild tree is a Gather target priced by the same yield model as grass (at least grass's rate on that ground), or the contract stops saying "fell trees".
- **A162 (F10). Hostiles are chasers and patrollers, not guards.** Replace the post-and-reach `Sighting` model with the published one: a chaser closes on the nearest character on its map by greedy steps and stops where no step closes in (so a still hostile is blocked, not guarding), a zone spawn keeps to its zone, a patroller keeps its loop; a hostile's damage taken is kept until it dies or its respawn; safety and hazard costs are judged on the exact safe block.
- **A163 (F11). The boss clock is counted, and a key is spent on every pass.** The fight's clock starts on the tick the step through the boss door applied, against the level's limit kept in the knowledge base (5 minutes until one is learned); near the limit Boss leaves through the room's exit door, and a boss's damage taken makes a return worth weighing. `boss_fight_end_tick` retires. `level_order.way_out` prefers a way out that spends no key, and a locked level the plan means to come back to reserves a spare.
- **A164 (F12). Retire the position read's `level`.** `WorldModel.apply_position` stops reading a `level` no position read carries (A37's branch).
